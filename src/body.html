      How Bite Rate works

      1. Pick a cut. Date night, first date, a catch-up with friends, fika, wine. Tap one to filter the map and the list.
      2. Say GO or SKIP. One vote per place. Tap again to take it back.
      3. Suggest what we missed. Make your case in a line or two. A pink ? pin is a suggestion in the queue.
      4. At +5 net votes shared suggestions become eligible for review. When shared voting is unavailable, votes and suggestions stay on this device.
      5. Open in Google Maps before you go. Hours and menus change.
